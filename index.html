Something else? Ask us

Do I need to be home during visits?

No. As long as we can get into the garden, we'll get on with it and send a short photo report when we're done.

Are you insured?

Yes. We carry full public liability insurance and certificates are available on request. Every team member is trained and DBS-checked.

What happens to the garden waste?

All green waste is removed and composted or recycled through licensed facilities. It's included in every quote.

Can I pause or cancel a care plan?

Yes. Plans are rolling with no long-term contract. Pause over winter or cancel with two weeks' notice.
